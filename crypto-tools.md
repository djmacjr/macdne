# Crypto Tools

This page exists for one purpose:

**Decide whether panic conditions are ending — and whether action is justified.**

No predictions. No hype. No intraday reacting.

**Score:** 0–10
**Last updated:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Bias:** Preserve capital. Observe only. / Controlled entry only.

---

## Panic Reversal Checklist (10-Point)

### 1) Market Structure (0–3)

* [ ] BTC has not made a new low in the last 48 hours
* [ ] BTC is trading sideways (range-bound) on the daily chart
* [ ] Daily candles are smaller than the panic candles

### 2) Volume & Volatility (0–2)

* [ ] Highest volume day is in the past
* [ ] Daily ranges are compressing (volatility cooling)

### 3) Relative Strength (0–2)

* [ ] ETH is no longer underperforming BTC
* [ ] A “quality” alt (choose 1) is stabilizing (not making new lows)

### 4) Liquidations / Leverage (0–2)

* [ ] 24h liquidations are down 50%+ from the peak day
* [ ] Open interest is flat or falling while price stabilizes

### 5) Headlines / System Risk (0–1)

* [ ] No fresh systemic event in the last 24 hours (exchange failure, major stablecoin break, regulatory bomb)

---

## Score Interpretation

**0–5:** Panic ongoing → NO-GO
**6–7:** Transition zone → WATCH
**8–10:** Conditions improving → GO (controlled)

---

## If Status = GO (Simple Execution Plan)

**Rule 1:** No “all-in” entries.
**Rule 2:** Enter in 3 tranches (small → medium → small).
**Rule 3:** If price breaks the recent low, stop buying and return to NO-GO.

**Example (Replace with your number)**

* Tranche 1: 25%
* Tranche 2: 50% (only if stability holds 24–48h)
* Tranche 3: 25% (only if trend improves on daily)

---

## Daily Ritual (3 Minutes)

1. Check BTC daily candle (range vs new low)
2. Check ETH/BTC relative strength
3. Check liquidations + open interest
4. Update the GO/NO-GO box
5. If not clearly GO → do nothing
